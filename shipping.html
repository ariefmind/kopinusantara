Wholesale & Export

Shipping & export, made straightforward.

Everything a roaster or importer needs to know before placing a first order — Incoterms, minimum order quantities, packaging, and lead times.

Trade terms

Incoterms
FOB / CIF
FOB Jakarta or Belawan as standard; CIF available on request for select destinations.
Minimum order
1 x 19.2 MT FCL
Smaller trial orders (LCL, from 60kg) available for new roaster partners.
Lead time
3–5 weeks
From confirmed order to vessel departure, depending on origin and season.
Payment terms
T/T or L/C
30% deposit, 70% against shipping documents is standard for new accounts.

Packaging options

GrainPro-lined jute bags

60kg jute sacks with a GrainPro barrier bag for moisture and freshness control on long ocean transit — our default for full-container orders.

Vacuum-sealed cartons

1kg or 5kg vacuum-sealed units in cartons, suited to LCL and sample-volume orders for roasters.

Custom private label

Retail-ready bags with your branding, available for larger recurring orders — ask for lead time and minimums.

How ordering works

01

Inquiry & sample

Tell us the origin, volume, and timeline you're after. We'll send cupping samples for evaluation.

02

Contract & deposit

Once you confirm, we issue a proforma invoice and lock in the lot at agreed pricing and terms.

03

Prep & documentation

We prepare Certificate of Origin, phytosanitary certificate, and other export documents required by your country.

04

Shipment & tracking

Container is booked and shipped; you receive tracking details and full shipping documents before arrival.
